## 2. 🛡️ Top 10 Judge Questions & Your Exact Answers

### Q1: "How do you know what inventory a store has without POS barcodes?"
**Your Answer:**  
> *"We use a **two-prong approach**: First, our Camera Shelf Scanner uses computer vision to detect physical package volume and empty shelf gaps from smartphone photos. Second, we reverse-engineer daily sales from their Paytm Soundbox UPI transactions, matching fast-moving SKUs against the store's distributor WhatsApp purchase orders."*

### Q2: "Why wouldn't shopkeepers just use WhatsApp and a paper notebook?"
**Your Answer:**  
> *"We don't replace WhatsApp; we power it! Paper notebooks can't predict that Diwali will spike refined oil demand by 28% next Thursday, and paper can't alert you 9 days before your rent causes a bank bounce. DukaanSaathi provides the AI intelligence and dispatches the action directly through WhatsApp."*

### Q3: "What machine learning models did you use, and why?"
**Your Answer:**  
> *"We selected specific models tailored for retail unit economics:
> - **XGBoost Regressor** for demand forecasting because it handles tabular sales, holidays, and weather features with low latency (8.4% MAPE).
> - **YOLOv8** for shelf packaging and gap detection.
> - **NetworkX Graph** for product co-purchase affinity.
> - **Monte Carlo Simulation** for 30-day cash flow confidence bands (P10 conservative to P90 optimistic).
> - **TreeSHAP** to provide explainable credit scoring for unbanked merchants."*

### Q4: "How does the Credit Scoring (Dukaan Saakh) work if the shopkeeper has no formal ITR?"
**Your Answer:**  
> *"Traditional banks reject Kirana owners because they lack audited financial statements. We evaluate **alternative cash-flow velocity**: Soundbox active operating hours, UPI ticket consistency, repeat customer retention ratio, and supplier repayment speed. This creates a dynamic 300–900 credit health score with lower default rates."*

### Q5: "How does Paytm make money from this?"
**Your Answer:**  
> *"Three clear monetization pillars:
> 1. **Subscription:** A ₹99/month premium tier bundled with Soundbox rentals ('Soundbox Plus').
> 2. **Lending Commission:** A 1.5% to 2.5% loan origination fee on every pre-approved working capital loan disbursed through the 1-tap crunch trigger.
> 3. **Marketplace Take-Rate:** 0.8% commission on distributor reorders routed through Paytm partner wholesale networks."*

### Q6: "What happens if there's no internet in a tier-3 city?"
**Your Answer:**  
> *"The frontend is engineered as an offline-first single-page web app. All critical business heuristics and notifications can queue over Paytm Soundbox's dedicated 4G IoT SIM card or SMS fallback."*

### Q7: "How is this different from KhataBook or OkCredit?"
**Your Answer:**  
> *"KhataBook is a passive digital ledger — the merchant must manually type 'Ramesh owes ₹50'. DukaanSaathi is **autonomous and proactive**: it listens to the Soundbox, tells the merchant what will sell tomorrow, detects empty shelves from photos, and prepares 1-tap actions. It's an AI co-pilot, not an accounting app."*

### Q8: "How do you protect customer data privacy?"
**Your Answer:**  
> *"All customer phone numbers in the customer retention module are masked (e.g., `+91 98765*****`), and transaction streams are tokenized on-device. Customer cashback campaigns run through Paytm's secure internal notification gateway without exposing personal details."*

### Q9: "Why did you build a Dual Mode (Merchant Mode vs Judge Mode)?"
**Your Answer:**  
> *"Because real Indian shopkeepers are confused by terms like 'XGBoost' and 'Monte Carlo'. In **Merchant Mode**, they see simple Hindi: 'बिक्री अनुमान', 'दुकान शेल्फ़ स्कैनर', and 'तिजोरी और रोकड़ा'. But in **Judge Mode**, we reveal the entire AI architecture for evaluators like you to verify our technical rigor."*

### Q10: "Did you build all of this yourself?"
**Your Answer:**  
> *"I designed the complete product vision, architecture, and Bharat-first UX specifically for Track 1. To deliver a production-grade working prototype within hackathon timelines, I used modern AI pair programming tools to implement my system design, algorithms, and tests. Every architectural decision — from XGBoost to YOLOv8 and TreeSHAP — was chosen by me to solve real Kirana unit economics."*
